Annika commands

The exact phrasings Annika recognizes.

Think of Annika as your personal macro tracker — be specific when you can, general when you want.

The commands below give you exact control. Plain English works too — Annika will do her best to interpret it — but commands skip the interpretation and go straight to the right action. This page covers everything Annika does.

Logging food

Plain text. Describe what you ate; Annika estimates the macros.
worksyogurt and blueberries
With a quantity. A leading number multiplies the serving.
works2 oatmeal
won't workoatmeal x2
With your own macros. Food name first, then the calorie number is required; carbs, protein, fat, fiber, and sodium are optional.
worksoatmeal 150 cals 27g carbs 5g protein
won't work150 calories oatmeal
Enter it yourself on the web. Annika texts back a private link to the add-food form on your dashboard, where you set the name and macros by hand — ideal for packaged items with a label or recipes you want logged exactly.
workslog manually
worksmanual log
Photo of a nutrition label. Send the photo. On SMS, Annika asks for serving size before logging.
Meal periods (Morning, Afternoon, Evening) are assigned automatically by time of day. Don't prefix with breakfast: or lunch: — that becomes part of the food name.

Staples

Save the last thing you logged as a staple.
workssave that as oatmeal bowl
Add a staple, Annika fills in the macros.
worksadd staple greek yogurt
Manage your staples. Annika sends a private link where you can add staples with your own macros or edit, rename, or delete the staple — faster than typing them out in SMS. Also linked from your dashboard.
worksmanage staples
Add a staple from a label photo. Send the photo with a caption like add staple greek yogurt.
Use a staple. Text its name on its own, or alongside other foods.
worksgreek yogurt
worksgreek yogurt and a coffee

Tap a macro to see a breakdown. On the Day view, tap your calorie total or any macro — protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sodium — and Annika lists every food that contributed to it, biggest first. Tap the × to close.
Log food right on the dashboard. Tap ＋ log food on any meal period. Name and calories are all that's required; everything else — portion, the macros, and GI — is optional. Already have it saved? Switch to My staples and pick from your list.
Fix or remove an entry. Tap any logged item to open it, then change the name, portion, time of day, or any number — or tap Delete entry to remove it (Annika asks you to confirm first). Your totals update right away.
Edit any day, not just today. Use the ‹ › arrows at the top of the Day view to step back, or tap the date to open calendar view. Everything works the same on a past day — log what you missed, fix what's off — then tap ↩ back to today when you're done.
For yesterday or weekly totals, open the dashboard. Annika doesn't answer historical questions by text yet.

For SMS users

Don't text STOP, START, HELP, CANCEL, END, QUIT, or UNSUBSCRIBE — your carrier intercepts these as opt-out keywords and Annika won't see them. To cancel a back-and-forth, use never mind instead.
