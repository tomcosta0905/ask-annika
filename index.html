How it works

What Annika does.

01

Your usuals become staples.

Save a food — or a combination of foods — once, and log it by name from then on. Your personal database, built as you go.

save as staple my usual breakfastmy usual breakfast
02

Staples go together.

Text several in one message and each one logs exactly — no re-entering, no guessing.

apple and my usual breakfast and siggis yogurt
03

Tell Siri.

Save Annika as a contact and Siri can text her for you — screen off, eyes on the road. It's reliable in a way voice usually isn't, because the words are your own staple names.

Siri, text Annika my usual breakfast and an apple
04

Packaged foods: show the label.

Snap the nutrition label and she logs it exactly — no tilde. It's also the quickest way to save a new staple. Prefer to type it in? Enter it by hand on the web.

photo of labellog manually
05

Anything else: describe it.

A new food, a restaurant plate, a one-off — say it the way you'd say it. Annika estimates, and marks every estimate with a tilde, so a guess always looks like a guess.

chicken marsala and a salad

Annika's math is as good as your description. When it needs to be exact, show her the label.

The dashboard

Step back to any day to fix what you missed, switch to the week and tap any day for its details, or open the month to see the bigger picture — and which foods built it.
